Responsible AI Log - Human Corrections

Below are the 5 cases where the AI diagnosis was corrected by a human expert, demonstrating the necessity of human oversight.

Case 08 - Trunking vs VLAN Mismatch

AI Said: VLAN mismatch on switch port (Confidence: 70%)

Human Corrected: Trunk link incorrectly configured as access port

Why AI Failed: AI ignored the empty show interfaces trunk output and guessed a VLAN assignment issue.

Case 12 - Encapsulation Mismatch

AI Said: VLAN 10 was deleted from switch database (Confidence: 80%)

Human Corrected: Router subinterface encapsulating wrong VLAN ID (99 instead of 10)

Why AI Failed: AI confused a Layer 2 switch issue with a Layer 2/3 router encapsulation issue.

Case 17 - Blackhole Route

AI Said: Static route for VLAN 20 is missing (Confidence: 60%)

Human Corrected: Traffic routed to Null0 (Blackhole route detected)

Why AI Failed: AI failed to understand that Null0 is a virtual blackhole interface that drops traffic.

Case 22 - DHCP Exhaustion

AI Said: DHCP server service is turned off (Confidence: 50%)

Human Corrected: DHCP excluded-address range covers entire subnet

Why AI Failed: AI defaulted to "DHCP is down" without reading the excluded-address configuration.

Case 26 - ACL Block vs Physical Down

AI Said: Router interface is physically down (Confidence: 40%)

Human Corrected: Inbound ACL explicitly blocking ICMP traffic

Why AI Failed: AI guessed Layer 1 issue while Layer 4 ACL evidence was clearly present.
